Privacy Policy — Fruit Learning Games for Kids

Last updated: October 31, 2026 • Developer: Maaz • Package: com.maaz.fruitlearninggames • Contact: FruitLearningGames20069@gmail.com

Fruit Learning Games for Kids ("the App", package com.maaz.fruitlearninggames) is an educational game designed for children ages 3 to 6, developed by Maaz. We respect your privacy and the privacy of your child. This policy explains what information the App does and does not collect.

Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no forms asking for names, emails, photos, or location. Game progress (such as stars and stickers) is stored only on your device and never leaves it.

Advertising

The App shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Because the App is designed for children, advertising is configured as child-directed: personalized advertising and remarketing are disabled, and only ads appropriate for families from Google's certified ad program are shown. AdMob may collect device information (such as an advertising identifier) strictly to serve and measure these non-personalized ads, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Google Play Families Policy.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It contains no chat, no social features, no external links accessible to children, and no in-app purchases in the children's sections. External links (such as rating the app) are placed only behind a parents' gate protected by a simple math question.

Third-Party Services

Other than Google AdMob described above, the App uses no third-party analytics or tracking services.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
